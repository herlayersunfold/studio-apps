Canvas Size
Tumbler wrap = the flat, unrolled label area (width is the circumference, height is the tall side). These are fixed grids at the smallest size (2.8mm) — always compare against your specific tumbler blank before ordering, since sizes vary by brand. Switching to a different size will recompute the grid for the same physical size.
Duplicate & Resize
Keeps the same design and colors — only recalculates the drill grid for a new size. Doesn't re-analyze the photo, so nothing shifts or re-colors.
Type one size, then tap a button to fill in the other so the new canvas has exactly the same shape as your current pattern — nothing stretched and no extra sides.
Keep proportions (fit inside)
Off = current behavior (the design stretches to fill the new size). On = the design keeps its true shape and nothing is cropped; the leftover space is filled with the picture's outer edge colors.
Match the original photo
Re-reads your uploaded photo at the new size, using only the colors already in your pattern, so it looks as close to the original as possible. Needs the photo to still be loaded. Works with or without Keep proportions.
Keep my hand edits
Only used with Match the original photo. Each new cell picks the photo's closest color, but only from colors already in that same spot of your edited pattern, so your recolors and cleanup stay. Turn off to read purely from the photo (edits are dropped).
Use a new-size photo (optional). Have a version of your picture already sized or cropped to the NEW shape? Upload it here. The resize reads that photo, keeps your pattern's original colors, and, if Keep my hand edits is on, keeps your edits. The tool lines your edited pattern up with the new photo automatically (it needs to be the same picture, just sized or framed differently). If it can't line them up, it builds from the photo and your original colors instead and tells you.
Trace Over Photo
Shows your uploaded photo behind the pattern so you can compare colors and repaint cells by hand. Reference only — it never changes your pattern, exports, or PDF.
