STUDIO APPS - HOW TO PUT THEM ON GITHUB PAGES
=============================================
This folder holds two installable apps (build 2026-10-10e):
  diamond-dander/   Diamond Dander Art Studio
  canvas-studio/    Canvas Studio

BEFORE YOU START: back up your saved projects
  Saved projects live inside the browser, tied to the web address.
  A new web address starts empty. In your CURRENT copy of the studio, open
  "Saved Projects & Backup", download a backup, and keep that file safe.
  After the new app is up, open it and restore the backup there.

STEP 1  Make a free account at github.com (skip if you have one).
STEP 2  Click the + at the top right, then "New repository".
        Name it something short like  studio  (the name becomes part of the web address).
        Choose PUBLIC (free GitHub Pages needs a public repository).
        Click "Create repository".
STEP 3  On the new repository page, click "uploading an existing file".
        Unzip this download first, then drag the two folders
        (diamond-dander and canvas-studio) into the upload box. Wait until every file
        has finished uploading, then click "Commit changes".
STEP 4  Click Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to
        "Deploy from a branch", pick the branch "main" and folder "/ (root)", click Save.
STEP 5  Wait a minute or two, refresh the Pages screen. It shows your address:
          https://YOURNAME.github.io/studio/diamond-dander/
          https://YOURNAME.github.io/studio/canvas-studio/
STEP 6  Install it:
          iPad / iPhone (Safari): open the address, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
          Computer (Chrome or Edge): open the address, click the install icon at the right
          end of the address bar.
        Open it once while online. After that it also works with no internet.

UPDATING LATER
  When you get a new version, replace index.html AND sw.js inside the app folder
  (upload them over the old ones). The next time the app opens online, it updates itself.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Anyone who has the address can open the app, and its code is visible. Do not put
    anything private in the repository.
  - The app's own fonts come from Google; with no internet it falls back to plain fonts.
    Everything else works offline, including PDF export.
